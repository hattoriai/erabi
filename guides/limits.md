# Known limits

What Erabi does not do yet. Each is a good first issue to open.

* **Phones.** The popup is a dropdown on every screen; a bottom sheet on
  narrow screens (as Koyomi's) is to come, and the pickers are not tested
  on phones.
* **Screen readers.** The roles and announcements are tested in three
  browsers; not yet with VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS themselves.
* **Item-aligned lists.** A select's list opens under the field; the
  list placed over the field with the chosen option aligned (macOS) is to
  come.
* **Editing options.** Renaming, recoloring and reordering options in
  the list (Notion's) is to come; options are the app's.
* **Option rows of the app's own.** Rows are drawn by Erabi (a label, an
  avatar or icon, a hint, a badge); a slot for whole rows is to come.
* **Scripts without spaces.** Matching finds words by spaces and
  punctuation; Japanese and Chinese names match by their letters in
  order, not by words.
* **Collaboration.** A shared picker (with Issho) is to come.
